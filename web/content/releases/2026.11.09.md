## Release Highlights (`2026.11.09`)

- **Calamares Bare-Metal & Ventoy UnpackFS Fix (`enerco-enterprise-tools 1.0.0-18`):**
  - Resolved `Bad unpackfs configuration: The source filesystem "/run/archiso/bootmnt/arch/x86_64/airootfs.sfs" does not exist` occurring during installation on physical laptops with > 4.8 GB RAM and Ventoy USB boot setups.
  - Added `copytoram=n` kernel parameters to UEFI (`efiboot/loader/entries/`) and Syslinux BIOS (`syslinux/archiso_sys-linux.cfg`) bootloader configurations to prevent Archiso from unmounting the ISO boot medium (`/run/archiso/bootmnt`).
  - Integrated dynamic pre-flight resolver `/usr/local/bin/enerco-prepare-unpackfs` executed automatically by `enerco-live-init` and `enerco-installer` prior to Calamares launch. The resolver automatically detects, loopback-mounts, or symlinks `airootfs.sfs` across Ventoy, standard `dd`, copy-to-RAM, and virtual machine topologies, and dynamically rewrites Calamares' `unpackfs.conf` if an alternate mount path is active.
- **Valid 1080p Wallpaper Integrity (`enerco-branding 1.0.0-9`):**
  - Re-encoded and verified the complete 8-byte PNG header and stream integrity of `/usr/share/backgrounds/enerco-mac.png` (1920x1080).
  - Fixed swaybg background image loading failure on both live ISO sessions and installed workstations, restoring the signature deep midnight and cobalt aurora wallpaper.
- **Packaging Deconfliction:**
  - Removed duplicate unmanaged `/usr/local/bin/enerco-prepare-unpackfs` from `airootfs`, centralizing ownership and maintenance strictly inside the `enerco-enterprise-tools` Arch package.
